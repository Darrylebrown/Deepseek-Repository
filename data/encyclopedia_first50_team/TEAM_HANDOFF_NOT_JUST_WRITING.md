## How we survive the beast (waves, not chaos)

**Do not** open all 8 languages + audio + 2,000 pins on day one.

### Suggested order (owner strategy)
1. **Stabilize EN source volumes** (clean, packable, series-consistent)  
2. **EN discoverability** (metadata, covers, site, light pins)  
3. **Libraries / institutional path** (high $ per book — this is a main economic spine)  
4. **One translation wave at a time** (e.g. **ES for all 50** before FR/PT/…)  
5. **Audio only on proven titles** — human narration  
6. **Pins per live edition**, not pins for phantom languages  

Batching is discipline. “We’ll just do everything” is how projects die.
