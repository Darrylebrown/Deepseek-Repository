## Required Dedication (every volume)

**Dedication**

This volume is dedicated to the Gullah Geechee people — past, present, and future — whose resilience, creativity, and deep cultural knowledge have sustained a living heritage across centuries. May this work honor your ancestors, support your communities, and contribute to the preservation and celebration of your culture for generations to come.

## Standard Volume Structure (exact order)

1. Dedication  
2. Introduction (~1,000 words) — Scope, why it matters, overview  
3. Historical Background / Origins (~2,500 words) — African roots, Lowcountry development, key periods  
4. Key Themes / Core Content (~5,000 words) — Main analysis, practices, meanings, structures  
5. Case Studies / Examples (~2,000 words) — People, families, places, artifacts, communities  
6. Contemporary Relevance (~1,000 words) — Current status, challenges, ongoing significance  
7. Conclusion (~500 words) — Summary and forward-looking reflection  
8. References & Further Reading (~1,000 words) — Academic sources and recommended readings  

**Word budget check:** 1000+2500+5000+2000+1000+500+1000 ≈ 13,000 body+refs (dedication separate) — aim ~12k core scholarly body per owner target; trim core content if needed to hit pack limits.
