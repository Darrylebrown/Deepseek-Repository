## When a volume lands

1. Drop MS into `incoming/vol-NN-slug/` (or Slack path).
2. Computer brand-gates + structure-checks against brief.
3. Build pack in `packaged/vol-NN-slug/`:
   - `MANUSCRIPT.txt` (final)
   - `METADATA.md` (from template)
   - `KDP_PASTE.md`
   - `manuscript.epub`
   - `COVER_FINAL.jpg` (1600×2560 or better)
   - `QA_CHECKLIST.md` (signed)
4. Update `VOLUME_INDEX.csv` status → `bookshelf_ready`
5. Append line to root `BOOKSHELF_READY.md`
6. Slack: `READY · encyclopedia vol NN · path`
